Deck — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Deck is a personal dashboard that Maged Hennawy runs on his own Mac. It isn't offered to anyone else, and only his own Google account is connected to it.

What Deck accesses

  • Gmail, read-only. Deck reads messages in his Gmail account to find things that need his action, such as bills, pickups, appointments and replies.
  • Gmail labels and filters. When he asks it to, Deck creates a "Noise" label and filters that move chosen senders out of his inbox. It can't send, delete or forward email.

How the data is used

  • To show him reminders on his dashboard, and to help him unsubscribe from or hide senders he chooses.
  • To decide whether an email needs action, a trimmed copy of its text (never attachments) is checked by Claude, made by Anthropic, under his own account. Mail from Government of Canada senders is never sent.
  • Data from Gmail is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with anyone else, and never used to train AI models.

What is stored, and where

  • Only short reminder details: sender, subject, date, a one-line reminder and a link back to the email in Gmail. They're stored on his Mac. Email bodies aren't stored.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on his Mac.

Google API Services

Deck's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turning it off

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Your connections to third-party apps & services. Removing it stops Deck from reading mail. The reminder details on the Mac can be deleted along with the app.
